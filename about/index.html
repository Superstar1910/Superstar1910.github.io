ABOUT ME

Building at the intersection of capital, infrastructure and technology.

I’m Kenneth S. Legesi — an investment professional and engineer interested in the systems, institutions and practical tools that enable people and businesses to create lasting value.

MY BACKGROUND

Two disciplines. One way of thinking.

My academic training is in civil and environmental engineering, with both bachelor’s and master’s degrees. I am also a CFA charterholder. The combination shapes how I approach a challenge: understanding the physical, financial and institutional system together.

As CEO and Chief Investment Officer of Oakstone Capital (formerly Ortus Africa Capital), I work across investment research, corporate and project finance, private capital, capital-market development and infrastructure. I am interested not only in structuring capital, but in the conditions that make investment productive and sustainable.
