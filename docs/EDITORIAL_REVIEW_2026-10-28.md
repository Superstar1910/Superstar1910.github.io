# Editorial and navigation review — 28 October 2026

## Article coverage

Before this review, the site contained 46 locally hosted publications dated
2025–2026. The expanded register contains **72 entries: 55 native full-text
publications and 17 external-only articles**, spanning 2018, 2020, 2021, 2024,
2025 and 2026. A year appears only when an actual dated record exists.

Medium's author RSS supplied 10 entries: nine complete bodies were reproduced
and one excerpt-only entry links to the original. LinkedIn public search verified
19 article URLs and their edition dates. Sixteen are new external entries; three
are linked as alternate editions of articles already in the register. No search
snippet is presented as a reproduced full article.

**This is not a complete-platform certification.** Medium's latest-ten feed may
omit older work. LinkedIn's complete article listing and full-text export were
unavailable. Its profile also lists *Dr. Geoffrey Alan Onegi-Obel: A Life of
Building Markets* dated 27 October 2026, but a verified article URL/full text was
not retrieved, so no invented page or URL was published. Substack's archive and
feed returned a Site Unavailable document in this environment; the existing
reviewed author-exported content was retained. Complete author exports are needed
to close these remaining gaps. See `ARTICLE_RECONCILIATION.json` for item records.

## Organisation

Writing now starts with the searchable article archive and filters by year,
topic and platform. Full-text/external status is explicit. Research repository
and Related work flags expose relationships without competing directory sections.
The former Learning Collection and Original Series blocks no longer precede it.

Financial Literacy is one education project in Work, with its planner beneath it
at `/work/financial-literacy/emergency-fund/`. Its publications also appear in
Writing and link back to the project. Old learning URLs remain redirects.

Work has search plus category filtering. All 21 entries open their own internal
landing pages; repository links belong on those project pages. Shared navigation
remains sticky while scrolling, with focus/anchor offsets on desktop and mobile.

## Contact

The exposed corporate email and direct mailto links are removed from published
pages. Contact CTAs and global Connect links use `/connect/`. The flow prepares a
message locally, lets the visitor copy it and opens the verified LinkedIn profile
for the visitor to send. Privacy requests have a dedicated topic. The interface
states that nothing is sent from the website; there is no fake submission success,
backend inbox, hidden email address or local-storage collection of the draft.

## RSS and publishing

The native feed is upgraded to full-text RSS with canonical links and preserved
publication dates/GUIDs. Every content page exposes RSS autodiscovery. Native
articles offer X/LinkedIn sharing links. RSS does not itself publish to other
accounts; no cross-posting credentials or API connections are configured.
Medium/Substack supported import workflows are documented in `PUBLISHING.md`.

## Verification

Publication, navigation, URL and editorial audits cover the full generated site.
CI also exercises sticky headers, old/new URLs, Work search, historical/platform
archive filters, contact draft preparation, calculator behaviour and no-JS
availability. The packaged artifact excludes imports, documentation and scripts.
