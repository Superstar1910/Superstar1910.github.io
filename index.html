01 / PERSPECTIVE

From the physical asset
to the financial model.

I’m the CEO and CIO of Oakstone Capital, building an investment and advisory platform across venture, private credit, infrastructure and real assets in Africa.

My background spans civil and environmental engineering (BSc, MSc) and investment management as a CFA Charterholder. It shapes how I approach a business: understand what it needs to work, then structure the capital and systems to support it.
