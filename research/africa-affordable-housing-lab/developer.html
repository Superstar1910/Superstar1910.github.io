Project assumptions (UGX)

Feasibility outputs

The illustrative model assumes all homes are sold at the same price. Grant and subsidy are deducted from project cost for scenario analysis; both require committed funding to be considered real. The margin is a markup on net costs, not project IRR. Monthly debt service is an indicative fully amortising repayment and not a construction-loan cash-flow model. No inflation, VAT, land title costs, working capital, timing, tax, cost of equity, sales absorption, or default modelled.
