Proposed architecture · roles are not appointments

MoFPED as policy host; UDB as proposed execution anchor; a National Capital Formation Council for coordination and accountability. Prior design contemplated Old Mutual as fund manager/adviser and Oakstone for strategy, structuring and implementation, subject to documented mandates and independent governance.

Institutional capital

Build investments worthy of pension money.

Prepare

Ring-fenced facility; independent feasibility, contracts, models and staged budgets.

Allocate risk

Priced, specific mitigation with transparent uncovered risks and fiscal limits.

Anchor

Independent cornerstone approvals and demonstrable risk-adjusted economics.

Eligibility, fiduciary capacity, market absorption and pipeline readiness are separate tests. Trustees choose voluntarily. URBRA retains regulatory independence; separately licensed managers underwrite and manage assets.

Pilot sequence

Seasoned infrastructure debt; diversified private credit/receivables; operating energy and industrial assets. Greenfield equity, PE and venture require later specialist capability.

Reproducible economics

Investment flows and preparation losses.

The September article path reproduces US$1,226.976bn cumulative gross capital formation and US$393.216bn above its conditional 24% comparison path during 2026–2040. These are scenario flows, not a funding commitment or measured external financing gap.

The inherited 2030 allocation totals US$7.2bn additional annual investment across nine ultimate-source channels. It remains a target mix to test against demand, fiscal capacity and actual project uses.

The synthetic preparation-facility model keeps failed studies and delayed recoveries visible. It demonstrates why a revolving facility cannot assume every cost is recovered at close.
