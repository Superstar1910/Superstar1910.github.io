Africa Affordable Housing Lab

From household incomes to feasible housing delivery: a public research initiative of Legesi.xyz.

Why this matters

Based on Kenneth Legesi's 2018 rent-to-own proposal: instead of starting with construction cost and hoping buyers can afford the homes, work backwards from household capacity to pay.

Explore a time-limited option to purchase a completed rental home, build rent credits toward a potential deposit, and assess indicative mortgage payments.

Interactive affordability explorer

Developer feasibility and blended finance

Test whether affordable selling prices can support land, construction, infrastructure and financing costs. Explore the viability gap and the role of grants or capital subsidies.
