RESEARCH PROGRAMME

Africa Investability Index

Economic opportunity is not the same as investability.

The Africa Investability Index (AII) is a developing research programme investigating how political institutions, domestic capital formation, market accessibility, enterprise durability and capital protection shape the conditions for productive investment across African economies.

Read the foundational essay: Economic Opportunity Is Not Investability →

Research questions

  1. Why do economies with comparable economic potential differ in their capacity to attract and sustain productive capital?
  2. Which institutional and financial mechanisms explain differences in investment execution and outcomes?
  3. Can a transparent framework improve investment decisions beyond existing country-risk and governance benchmarks?

Methodological status

The proposed dimensions are provisional. No country scores or rankings have been validated. Research will distinguish ex-ante investability, transaction feasibility and ex-post outcomes, and compare composite-index, dashboard and empirical approaches.

Editorial policy

Working papers and datasets are reviewed for provenance, originality, methodological integrity, licensing and factual accuracy before publication. The research programme is ongoing and does not constitute investment advice.

Research lead: Kenneth S. Legesi, CFA.
