Kenneth S. Legesi, CFA · 29 October 2026 · Research essay

Coffee Pricing Engine: From Yield per Tree to Buyer Quote

A public research programme and indicative calculator examining how costs accumulate from the productive coffee tree to an accepted kilogram of green coffee and a financially viable trade.

Explore the research

Read Coffee Is Traded, Not Just Grown and What Does a Kilogram of Coffee Really Cost?.

Try the prototype

Open the Coffee Pricing Engine. The app is an illustrative MVP: cost defaults are not independently verified Ugandan price data; it does not provide live executable market quotes. Grade conversions, historical transaction calibration and Incoterm-specific automated costing remain under development.

Our method

We separate farm production cost, trader procurement price, moisture- and grade-specific processing conversion, daily capital cost, physical coffee benchmark, buyer quote and realised netback. Inputs should retain source, observation date, geography, coffee form, quality and units. Core formulas and the research white paper are under validation; this page provides a public explanation of the programme without releasing private transaction data.

What evidence will help?

- Farm records with acreage, productive trees, harvests, costs and coffee form. - Actual milling input/output measurements, moisture, rejects, green bean outturn and grade split. - Dated anonymised physical buyer offers, freight and warehousing quotations, and financing terms. - Independent methodological review by farmers, processors, traders, academics and financial specialists.

How to contribute

Start a conversation describing the kind of evidence you can provide and its region/date/form. Do not send confidential customer or counterparty information, farm identities, unlicensed exchange feeds or proprietary prices without permission and an agreed evidence-sharing process. We will distinguish published facts, individual observations and illustrative assumptions, and recognise contributions where authorised.

Research stage

White paper and formula register: working draft. Uganda empirical calibration: not yet complete. Independent model and publication review: outstanding. Corrections and contrary evidence are expressly welcome.
