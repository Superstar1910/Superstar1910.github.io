The telecom, the fintech and the shareholder

A coverage framework guided by the published MTN articles: what is the asset worth, who owns the upside, and when can it become cash?

Prepared 13 October 2026 · Kenneth S. Legesi, CFA

Research status: Synthesis of published articles and a framework for ongoing coverage. Historical article claims have not been independently refreshed against current issuer filings. No current rating, target price or market quotation is asserted.

The investment question

The research sequence asks whether a telecom valuation obscures a financial-services asset, then whether the listed investor can actually capture that asset’s value. These are separate tests: attractive operating economics do not establish enforceable shareholder participation.

Value

Estimate telecoms and MoMo independently, with consistent earnings definitions and a reconciled enterprise-to-equity bridge.

Rights

Trace the listed share through the separation and any trust or FinCo entitlement. Verify distributions, control, dilution and exit.

Realisation

Model dividends, delays, partial monetisation and deterioration. A paper valuation gap is not an investor cash flow.

What would support or weaken the thesis?

Support would come from segment cash generation, transparent financial reporting and documented value-transfer mechanisms. Weakness would include adverse separation terms, capital leakage, dilution, sustained telecom deterioration or an exit path dependent on indefinite discretion.
