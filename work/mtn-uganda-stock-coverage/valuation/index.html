From sum of the parts to investor returns

A reproducible valuation pathway, with shareholder rights and realisation timing built into the equity bridge.

Prepared 13 October 2026 · Kenneth S. Legesi, CFA

Research status: Synthesis of published articles and a framework for ongoing coverage. Historical article claims have not been independently refreshed against current issuer filings. No current rating, target price or market quotation is asserted.

Value each business, then value the investor’s claim

The original fintech article motivates a telecoms/MoMo sum-of-the-parts approach. Transaction and public-market comparisons provide reference points, but differences in geography, margins, growth, regulatory scope and control require explicit adjustments.

StepRequired calculationKey control
TelecomCash-flow valuation and comparable earnings multiplesSeparate MoMo contributions and intercompany charges.
MoMoCash-flow valuation and relevant fintech benchmarksDo not treat transaction throughput as revenue; distinguish EV from equity value.
FinancingDebt, cash, leases and other claimsApply consistent lease treatment; avoid double deductions.
Shareholder entitlementValue of rights attached to the purchased securityVerify record dates, transferability and dilution before adding FinCo value.
ReturnDated distributions and exit proceeds against purchase costInclude timing, tax assumptions, liquidity and currency effects.

Scenario architecture

Full unlock, partial unlock, status quo and deterioration should use explicit cash flows. Do not assume the original probabilities or return ranges remain valid. Calculate scenario IRRs separately and probability-weight terminal wealth or NPV at a stated horizon; an average of IRRs is only a descriptive approximation.

Price discipline

The embedded-option argument is strongest when conservative value of the core business supports the entry price. That downside support still depends on operating resilience and the balance sheet. A valuable subsidiary does not make the telecom floor guaranteed.
