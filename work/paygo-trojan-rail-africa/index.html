The research question

Did PayGo solar companies merely become lenders, or did they build an origination, servicing, collections and device-control infrastructure capable of financing smartphones, productive-use equipment, mobility, clean cooking and smart utilities?

The central finding

The durable asset is not the solar panel or the lending spread in isolation. It is the closed-loop operating rail connecting trusted distribution, alternative underwriting, controllable collateral, mobile-money collections, field servicing and auditable portfolio reporting.

Capital markets increasingly finance the receivables produced by that rail through warehouse facilities, local-currency debt and securitisation. The operating company nevertheless retains origination, servicing, warranty and customer-relationship risk.
