The Ghosts of Drawdowns Past, Present, and Yet to Come

Originally published on Substack: 2025-04-19 · Original publication ↗

"I wear the chain I forged in life," said Marley. Likewise, investors bear the burden of their drawdowns—unless they learn to manage them.

The markets reminded us—once again—that they don’t move in straight lines.

Last week brought a mix of investor jitters: sticky inflation data, renewed rate-hike concerns, and earnings volatility sent the S&P 500 wobbling, just as many were starting to feel comfortable. It wasn’t a crash by any means—but it was enough to stir up that old familiar feeling: “Should I be worried?”

It’s in these moments that looking back can be instructive. And few tools are better for perspective than a good old-fashioned drawdown chart.

This one, shared by Harald Berlinicke, CFA, captures just how deep and painful major market corrections can be.

To note:

  • The Global Financial Crisis? A brutal -56.8% slide.

  • The COVID-19 shock? A sharp -33.9% in a matter of weeks.

These aren’t just numbers on a chart. They’re the moments that made your stomach drop—the ones that test your plan, your patience, and sometimes your sleep.

Enter Dickens: The Ghosts of Drawdowns

If Charles Dickens had written A Christmas Carol for investors, the spirits wouldn’t warn of stinginess—they’d warn of poor risk management.

Because every investor, at some point, is visited by the ghosts of drawdowns:

  • 👻 The Ghost of Drawdowns Past (who never forgets a loss),

  • 👻 The Ghost of Drawdowns Present (navigating the market now), and

  • 👻 The Ghost of Drawdowns Yet to Come (looming in the uncertain future).

Each ghost reflects a different risk appetite—and a different way of managing the emotional and financial impact of market downturns.

So, whether you're cautious like Scrooge at the start, balanced like Bob Cratchit, or bold like a reformed Scrooge on Christmas morning, understanding your drawdown mindset is key to surviving and thriving through market cycles.

Let’s meet the ghosts.

👴 The Ghost of Drawdowns Past — The Conservative Investor

Who they are: Cautious, loss-averse, focused on capital preservation.
Strategy:

  • 🧱 Heavy in bonds and cash

  • 🛑 Uses stop-losses and exits early

  • 💰 Keeps large cash buffers

  • 🕰️ Short to medium-term horizon
    Motto: “Preserve at all costs.”

🎩 The Ghost of Drawdowns Present — The Moderate Investor

Who they are: Balanced, steady, seeking growth without recklessness.
Strategy:

  • ⚖️ Diversified between stocks and bonds

  • 🔄 Rebalances regularly

  • 🏦 Maintains modest cash buffer

  • ⏳ Medium to long-term horizon
    Motto: “Stay steady, stay sane.”

🎁 The Ghost of Drawdowns Yet to Come — The Aggressive Investor

Who they are: Bold, growth-focused, unfazed by volatility.
Strategy:

  • 🚀 Heavy in equities, alternatives, and growth assets

  • 💼 Uses tactical hedging, but rarely stops out

  • 🪙 Minimal cash—sees dips as buying opportunities

  • ⏳ Long-term or generational outlook
    Motto: “No risk, no reward.”

So… Which Ghost Walks With You?

Drawdowns aren’t just statistical dips—they’re emotional tests.
Knowing your risk appetite helps you design a portfolio you can live with—not just when things go well, but when the market turns cold.

"I will honour investing in my heart, and try to keep it all the year." — Not Dickens, but close.
